Work Suite

Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026.

This policy describes the Google connection and client reports in the Work Suite desktop application, including Worksheet.

Data used

Work Suite stores work records locally on the user's computer. When the user enables a client report, the app sends the selected client's completed unpaid work information to Google Sheets: project and work names, work type, known receipt and completion dates, recorded hours, amounts and currencies. Private comments, local folder paths, chat sources and attachments are not included in this report.

Google access

The app uses Google OAuth in a browser. It does not collect the user's Google password. The requested drive.file permission allows the app to create and maintain files used with the app, rather than unrestricted access to the user's Google Drive. The app reads the connected account's email address and account identifier to display the connection and prevent reports from being updated under the wrong account.

Use and sharing

Google account data is used only to connect the account and create, identify and update the reports requested by the user. The developer does not operate a report collection server. Report data is sent directly from the desktop application to Google's APIs. The app does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

Files are created in the connected user's Google Drive. Client sharing permissions are controlled by that user in Google Sheets; the app does not automatically make reports public or send client invitations. Google's handling of data is also governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Local storage and retention

The app stores account connection tokens in the operating system's protected local storage using Electron safeStorage. The local work database keeps report links, sync status and work records. Personal account tokens are never included in distributed application packages. Report tables remain in Google Drive until their owner deletes them.

Disconnecting and deletion

Use Disconnect in the application to remove the local Google connection tokens and pause report updates. This does not delete work records or existing Google files. Access can also be revoked in the Google Account permissions settings. Users can delete report files through Google Drive and remove local application data through their operating system.

Google API policy

Work Suite's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
